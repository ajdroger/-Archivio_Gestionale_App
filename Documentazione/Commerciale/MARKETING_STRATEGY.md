## 5. PAID ADVERTISING

### LinkedIn Ads (B2B Focus)
- **Budget**: €2.5K/mese
- **Targeting**: IT Managers, CTOs, CFOs, 50-500 employees, Italy
- **Ad Types**: Sponsored content, InMail
- **KPIs**: €50 cost per lead (CPL), 3% CTR

### Google Ads (Search)
- **Budget**: €3K/mese
- **Keywords**: "gestionale enterprise", "software gestione soci", "alternative zucchetti"
- **Landing Pages**: Dedicated per keyword intent
- **KPIs**: €60 CPL, 25% conversion rate (lead-to-demo)

### Retargeting
- **Budget**: €1K/mese
- **Platforms**: Google Display, Facebook/Instagram
- **Audience**: Website visitors non-converted (30-day window)
- **Creative**: Testimonial quotes, feature highlights

**Total Paid Budget Year 1**: €78K/anno
